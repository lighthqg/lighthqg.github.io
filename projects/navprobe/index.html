Endpoint success leaves questions open

NavProbe checks individual instruction requirements against the executed trajectory. Conventional metrics and instruction-level results are reported together so readers can see where they agree and where they diverge.

Whole
The original instruction and starting pose.
Judgement
An executable check for a grounded requirement, such as crossing the specified opening or stopping by the correct object.
Micro
A self-contained fragment of the instruction, evaluated from a validated reset pose using the matching Judgements.
